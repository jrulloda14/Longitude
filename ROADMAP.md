# Roadmap

Longitude is shaped by feedback. Nothing here is a promise or a date. Ideas move up when people ask for them; [open a feature request](https://github.com/jrulloda14/Longitude/issues/new/choose) to vote with your use case.

## Now: v0.1 preview

- Windows release and Mac preview
- Lower idle CPU in live mode (the animated "Live" indicator; see [measurements](docs/evidence/milestone-7/PULSE_RESULTS.md))
- Signed and notarized Mac build once Apple developer signing is set up
- Physical Mac testing

## Candidates after Milestone 7

### Saved meetings (local)

A simple list of the meetings you create or share in Longitude, kept on your computer. It is not a calendar and doesn't sync with one.

- Every shared or exported meeting is saved, for example "Weekly sync with Client A, Tue 9:00 AM".
- Selecting a saved meeting moves the clock to it, so everyone's local time shows again.
- A countdown in your time ("in 2 days · 9:00 PM for you").
- Daylight-saving warnings when a repeating meeting will shift for someone.
- Copy or re-export without re-entering the details.

**Deliberately out of scope:** a full calendar grid, and syncing with Google, Outlook or Apple calendars. Those would need accounts and internet access, which Longitude avoids. Calendar sync would only be considered as a separate, opt-in decision if many users ask for it.

**Status:** candidate, waiting on user feedback (proposed October 21, 2026).
